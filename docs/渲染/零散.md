## d$\omega_h$推导
由于

$$dS=r^2d\omega=r^2\sin\theta d\theta d\phi$$

则

$$d\omega_h=\sin\theta_hd\theta_hd\phi_h$$

立体角各向同性，不妨认为$\omega_i$在极轴处，则$d\omega_h$在$\theta$方向上，$\theta_h=\frac{1}{2}\theta_o$，$\phi$方向上$\phi_h=\phi_o$
故

$$d\omega_h=\frac{1}{4\cos\theta_h}\sin\theta_od\theta_od\phi_o=\frac{1}{4(o\cdot h)}d\omega_o$$
